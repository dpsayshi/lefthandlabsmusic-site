Who makes this

Left Hand Labs Music

Left Hand Labs Music is a one-person audio plugin shop in Minnesota. It makes a free practice plugin for guitarists and nine genre drum kits for Windows. The drum sounds are real acoustic recordings from open-licence sample libraries, credited by name, and the bass tones in the free plugin are built from my own bass recordings.
