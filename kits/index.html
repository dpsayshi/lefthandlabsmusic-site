Drum kits // VST3 + standalone // Windows

The menu

Nine genre drum kit plugins. Pick the genre instead of building the sound: each kit is tuned for its genre and has one big knob, plus an optional mixer, Tune knob, presets, MIDI map and a groove player for your own MIDI files. Every drum hit is a real recording, from open-licence libraries or (the kick, snare and toms of Leviathan Drag) made by Left Hand Labs Music, and credited by name.
