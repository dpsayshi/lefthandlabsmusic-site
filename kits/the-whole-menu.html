Every kit on the menu

Smokestack (70s rock), Leviathan Drag (doom / sludge), Sunset Riot (hair rock), Neon Gate (80s), Razorback (80s thrash), Crypt Engine (90s death metal), Ground Zero (slam), Meatgrinder (brutal death), Fractal Cathedral (prog death). Each one is tuned for its genre and has its own big knob, so you pick the genre instead of building the sound. Same layout in every kit: variants, presets, six faders, Tune, MIDI map, groove player. One launcher installs the kits you tick.

Features

  • All 9 kits as VST3 + standalone for Windows. Each kit installs and uninstalls on its own.
  • Nine genre knobs: SMOKE, WEIGHT, HAIRSPRAY, GATE, BITE, ROT, IMPACT, GRIND, SPIRE.
  • Same controls everywhere: kit variants, 5 presets, Tune ±3 st, Kick/Snare/Toms/Cymbals/Room/Master faders.
  • MIDI map menu and MIDI groove player in every kit. Bring your own MIDI.
  • Real open-licence recordings (CC BY 4.0 / CC0). Full credits in every plugin and kit folder.

System requirements

  • OS: Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit [MINIMUM WINDOWS VERSION: tested on Windows 10 22H2 (build 19045) only]
  • Host: A 64-bit VST3 host, or use the included standalone app. [TESTED DAWS]
  • Disk: Download about 1.2 GB for all 9 (LHL_All_Kits launcher); installed size [INSTALLED SIZE]
  • RAM / CPU: [RAM / CPU]
  • Mac / Linux: Not available [MAC WORDING, see FAQ]
  • Internet: Only to download. No activation.

Built from

DrumGizmo libraries DRSKit, MuldjordKit, Aasimonster2 and CrocellKit (CC BY 4.0), plus Big Rusty Drums by Karoryfer Samples (CC0) in three kits. Drum samples provided by DrumGizmo.org. Some kits share source recordings. Full credits.
