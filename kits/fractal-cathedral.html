What the plugin looks like

What it does

Progressive death metal drums with room to breathe. Clean, open and dynamic, with six toms from high rack to floor and a full cymbal set including chinas and splashes. Odd-time fills don't run out of drums. Turn SPIRE to grow the hall and add air on top. Keep it low for tight tech parts, open it up for the long, slow sections. No mic balancing, no reverb sends to build. Load it and play. Prog death, tech death, anything with a 7/8 bar.

Features

  • SPIRE knob: hall size and air on top.
  • Kit variants Natural and Raw, plus 5 presets (Default, Raw / dry, Tight, Roomy, Big and loud).
  • Tune knob (kick/snare/toms ±3 semitones) and six faders: Kick, Snare, Toms, Cymbals, Room, Master.
  • MIDI map menu (GM, merged alternates, pedal hat, flipped toms) and a groove player for your own MIDI files.
  • VST3 + standalone for Windows. Real open-licence recordings, credited inside the plugin.

System requirements

  • OS: Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit (tested on Windows 10)
  • Host: A 64-bit VST3 host, or use the included standalone app. Tested in REAPER.
  • Disk: Download about 135 MB (LHL_Fractal_Cathedral_1.0.0_Setup.exe)
  • Mac / Linux: Not available yet. Windows only for now.
  • Internet: Only to download. No activation.
