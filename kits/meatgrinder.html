What the plugin looks like

What it does

Brutal death metal drums made for blast beats. A dry, clicky, very tight kick and snare with short decay. Turn GRIND for hard saturation and upper-mid presence. That's the grinder. No trigger setup, no transient shaping, no ten-plugin snare chain. Load it, program the blasts, done. Brutal death, grind, slam-adjacent, anything fast and ugly. A mixer and Tune knob are there if you need them.

Features

  • GRIND knob: hard saturation and presence.
  • Kit variants Natural and Raw, plus 5 presets (Default, Raw / dry, Tight, Roomy, Big and loud).
  • Tune knob (kick/snare/toms ±3 semitones) and six faders: Kick, Snare, Toms, Cymbals, Room, Master.
  • MIDI map menu: GM, merge 35>36 and 40>38, pedal hat plays closed, toms flipped for pad layouts.
  • VST3 + standalone for Windows. Groove player for your own MIDI files. Credits inside the plugin.

System requirements

  • OS: Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit (tested on Windows 10)
  • Host: A 64-bit VST3 host, or use the included standalone app. Tested in REAPER.
  • Disk: Download about 98 MB (LHL_Meatgrinder_1.0.0_Setup.exe)
  • Mac / Linux: Not available yet. Windows only for now.
  • Internet: Only to download. No activation.
