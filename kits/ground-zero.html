What the plugin looks like

What it does

Slam drums tuned to hit like a wall. A very low, deep kick and a dead, low-tuned snare, short and dry, with the kit pre-tuned down. Turn IMPACT for a sub shelf at the bottom, heavy parallel squash and a little extra pitch drop on kick and snare. No sub-layer hunting, no bus compressor to set. Load it, slow the breakdown down, play. Slam, deathcore, beatdown, anything with a breakdown. The mixer's there if you want it.

Features

  • IMPACT knob: low-end slam, parallel squash, pitch drops.
  • Kit variants Natural and Raw [CONFIRM VARIANTS: sub / no-sub layer in 1.0?], plus 5 presets.
  • Tune knob (kick/snare/toms ±3 semitones) on top of the kit's built-in low tuning.
  • Six faders (Kick, Snare, Toms, Cymbals, Room, Master) and a MIDI map menu (GM, merged alternates, pedal hat, flipped toms).
  • VST3 + standalone for Windows. Groove player for your own MIDI files. Credits inside the plugin.
