What the plugin looks like

What it does

Doom and sludge drums, already tuned down. Huge, loose kick and toms with long decay, and a fat, low early-70s-style doom snare built from real top, bottom, overhead and room mics. The toms go down to a 45 Hz floor tom. Turn WEIGHT and it pulls the kit's pitch further down, piles on low-end mass and opens a long, dark room. No tuning session, no sidechain homework. Load it, set the tempo to slow, and play. The mixer and Tune knob are there if you want them.

Features

  • WEIGHT knob: pitch down, low-end mass, long dark room.
  • Kit variants Natural and Raw [CONFIRM VARIANT NAMES], plus 5 presets (Default, Raw / dry, Tight, Roomy, Big and loud).
  • Kick, snare and toms pre-tuned 2 semitones down. The Tune knob moves them a further ±3 semitones.
  • Six faders (Kick, Snare, Toms, Cymbals, Room, Master), a MIDI map menu and a MIDI groove player for your own files.
  • VST3 + standalone for Windows. Real open-licence recordings (CC BY 4.0 + CC0), credited inside the plugin.

A version of Leviathan Drag is one of the 4 kits inside the free practice plugin, Backing Track Plus.
